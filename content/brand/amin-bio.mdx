## Long Bio (Investors, Partners, Government, Whitepapers)

**Dr. Amin Beiranvand Pour serves as the Co-Founder & Chief Scientist of Scanminers**, providing the scientific foundation behind the company's advanced remote sensing and mineral exploration methodologies.

A leading researcher in geological remote sensing, spectral unmixing, hyperspectral analysis, and machine learning–assisted exploration, Dr. Amin has authored more than 300 peer-reviewed publications and has collaborated with geological surveys, universities, and exploration companies worldwide.

His scientific frameworks, multi-sensor fusion methods, and geospatial algorithms are operationalized within the Scanminers platform—enabling explainable, high-confidence critical mineral prospectivity assessments across regional to global scales.
