## Core Messages

- **The gap** – Traditional prospecting waits on scarce field crews and partial data.
- **Our solve** – Scanminers orchestrates orbital sensing, ML, and geologic expertise to surface investor-grade intelligence in days.
- **Outcome** – Leaders allocate capital sooner, mitigate ESG risk, and keep regulators in the loop from day one.
- **Credibility** – Cite the Payas Islahiye pilot, cobalt supply chain analysis, and lidar tailings monitoring studies.

### Message Ladder

1. **Category** – Subsurface intelligence for energy and mining.
2. **Value prop** – Faster, citation-backed recommendations that tie geology to execution.
3. **Proof** – Time-to-insight metrics, coverage stats, ESG overlays.
4. **CTA** – Scope a basin, request a proof kit, or subscribe to the weekly mineral briefing.
