## Imagery Guidance

- Use macro earth textures, topographic lines, and satellite composites.
- Pair human imagery with candid field coordination moments, not staged hardhats.
- Always include a caption that references the dataset or mission depicted.

## Layout Rhythm

1. Hero background: Midnight Basin with a low-opacity intelligence grid.
2. Secondary sections alternate between dark and Survey Clay surfaces.
3. Cards use 16px radius with 1px stroke in translucent teal.
4. Keep typography tight—short headlines, scannable body copy.
