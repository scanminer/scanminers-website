- **Dr. Amin Beiranvand Pour** — Co-Founder & Chief Scientist
- **Mahmood Asadi** — Co-Founder & Chief AI & Product Architect

All responses, content, emails, AI drafts, and system prompts must reflect:

- **Scientific authority** from Dr. Amin (methodologies, research validation, geoscience expertise)
- **Technical + product leadership** from Mahmood (AI strategy, platform architecture, UX, automation)
- A **unified GeoAI brand narrative** that positions Scanminers as both scientifically rigorous and operationally innovative

## Tone Pillars

1. **Confident, never cocky** – We are experts who speak plainly about what we know and what needs validation.
2. **Grounded in evidence** – Cite data sources, models, or pilots whenever possible.
3. **Collaborative** – Invite regulators, communities, and partners into the solution.
4. **Responsible urgency** – We emphasize speed because the planet needs it, not because of hype.

Use the examples above when training AI models or editing copy. If a new scenario emerges, add an example via the admin portal with before/after context.
