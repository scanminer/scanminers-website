## Channel Highlights

### Website
- Rotate proof points quarterly.
- Pair every CTA with a value prop and supporting stat.

### Email / Sales
- Keep copy under 120 words.
- Name the mineral or geography in the subject line.
- Close with a collaborative next step (scope review, co-draft brief, etc.).

### Analyst Relations
- Lead with market context, then product differentiation, then proof.
- Offer to share anonymized briefs as leave-behinds.
