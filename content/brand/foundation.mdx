# Why Scanminers Exists

We accelerate critical mineral projects by giving leaders a live, multi-sensor picture of what is in the ground and how to approach it responsibly. The work is fast, citation-backed, and tuned for boardrooms, regulators, and field crews alike.

## Decision Story Arc

1. **Reveal the urgency** – The world needs responsibly sourced critical minerals faster than manual exploration allows.
2. **Expose the gap** – Current workflows wait on slow, expensive crews with narrow coverage.
3. **Name our unfair advantage** – Scanminers orchestrates satellite intelligence, proprietary models, and automation to light up unseen opportunity.
4. **Show the responsible path** – Every insight contains environmental, cultural, and compliance guardrails so approvals arrive alongside opportunity.
5. **Call to action** – Move the highest-confidence targets from idea to execution with a smaller footprint and a provable audit trail.

## Proof Themes

- Coverage: millions of square kilometers processed monthly across high-priority regions.
- Speed: intelligence kits land in under 72 hours once an AOI is greenlit.
- Responsibility: each recommendation contains citations, provenance, and ESG overlays.
