## Project at a glance

The client asked Scanminers to run a remote sensing study over the Payas–İslahiye region in Southeast Turkey, a bauxite province with dense vegetation and complex geology. The goal was to identify new bauxite potential beyond known mines, and to produce fusion maps and target lists that could guide future fieldwork and drilling.

**Key outcomes:**

- **7** high-potential zones identified
- **183** high-priority target points pinpointed
- **33** existing mines mapped for context
- Regional-scale study covering the **Payas–İslahiye bauxite province**

---

## Challenge

The Payas–İslahiye region hosts significant bauxite mineralisation but is covered by dense vegetation and complex topography. Traditional mapping alone makes it difficult to see the full extent of the system or to prioritise ground for follow-up. The client needed a way to look across the region consistently, highlight where new bauxite might occur, and understand how potential zones relate to existing mines and geological controls.

**Key challenges:**

- Dense vegetation and rugged terrain obscure surface geology
- Existing mines and prospects provide clues, but don't show the full opportunity
- The client needed regional-scale visibility to focus limited exploration budgets

---

## Approach: multi-sensor fusion tuned to bauxite

Scanminers combined geological research on the local bauxite system with multi-sensor satellite data to build a set of evidence layers tailored to bauxite. These layers were then fused using a structured, explainable approach to produce prospectivity maps and ranked targets.

**What we did:**

1. Reviewed geology, mineralogical and structural context of the Payas–İslahiye bauxite province
2. Assembled a **multi-sensor satellite stack** (optical, radar, elevation, and other relevant sources) covering the study area
3. Derived **alteration and lithological indicators** relevant to bauxite (e.g. clay, carbonate and iron-rich signatures)
4. Mapped **structural and morphological features** (lineaments, slopes, terrain breaks) that can control bauxite accumulation
5. Applied a **fusion framework** to weight evidence layers and generate continuous prospectivity maps for bauxite

## Results: high-potential zones, target points and mine context

The fusion maps revealed patterns in alteration, structure and existing mining that are not obvious from any single dataset. They provided the client with a clear set of zones and points to prioritise in the next phase of exploration.

**Delivered outputs:**

- **Seven high-potential zones** for bauxite mineralisation were identified across the study area
- Within these zones, **183 high-priority target points** were pinpointed as potential sites for follow-up work
- **33 existing mines** were mapped and integrated into the analysis, providing essential context and validation for the detected patterns
- The outputs were delivered as **fusion maps, target lists with coordinates, and a structured report**, ready to load into GIS and use for planning
