## Objective

To delineate bauxite-rich zones in the Payas–İslahiye region of Southeast Turkey using a rapid, data-driven approach that minimizes initial fieldwork.

## Methodology

Our AI-MPM engine executed a multi-stage workflow consistent with our core technology stack:

1.  **Ingest & Harmonize**: We processed ASTER, Landsat-8, and PALSAR satellite data. This included radiometric and atmospheric corrections (QUAC, Log-Residual) to ensure data consistency.
2.  **AI & Statistical Fusion**: Key techniques included Directed Principal Component Analysis (DPCA) and Mixture-Tuned Matched Filtering (MTMF) to identify mineralogical signatures associated with bauxite. Structural features were extracted using ALE & FPCS.
3.  **Rank & Validate**: An Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) model was used to transparently weight the evidence layers, producing a final prospectivity map.

## Results

The analysis successfully identified:
- **7 high-potential zones** for targeted exploration.
- **183 priority points** with coordinates for field verification (within 500-1000m buffers).
- **33 known mines** were accurately mapped, validating the model's predictive power.

This case demonstrates the engine's capability to rapidly narrow down vast regions into actionable, high-probability targets, supporting faster and lower-impact mineral discovery.
