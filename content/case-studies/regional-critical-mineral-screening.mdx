## Context

This case study illustrates a **conceptual, anonymized example** of Scanminers' regional-scale prospectivity workflow. It represents the type of engagement and outcomes we deliver rather than a specific client project.

The scenario: an exploration company sought to screen a large region (~500 km²) for bauxite and associated critical minerals using remote sensing and GeoAI before committing significant field resources.

## Objective

- Screen hundreds of km² to identify high-potential target zones
- Rank targets by prospectivity score with explainable reasoning
- Validate model predictions against known mineral occurrences
- Deliver actionable intelligence to guide follow-up field programs

## Methods & Models

Our standard multi-sensor fusion workflow was applied across ~500 km²:

1. **Data Ingestion & Harmonization**
   - Optical satellite: Landsat-8 (30 m resolution), ASTER (15–90 m resolution, multispectral and thermal)
   - Radar: PALSAR (12.5 m resolution) for structural interpretation
   - Topography: SRTM DEM (30 m resolution) for slope, aspect, and lineament extraction
   - Geochemistry: Public stream sediment and soil sample data (>200 sample points)

2. **Feature Engineering**
   - Spectral indices for alteration detection (SWIR, TIR bands): NDVI, NDMI, Band Ratio Analysis
   - Topographic derivatives: slope, aspect, curvature, structural tensors
   - Geochemical pathfinder ratios and spatial interpolation (IDW, Kriging)
   - Structural lineament mapping using edge-detection algorithms (>150 km of mapped lineaments)

3. **AI-Driven Fusion**
   - Directed Principal Component Analysis (DPCA) for multi-sensor integration
   - Gradient boosting (XGBoost) and Random Forest models trained on 50+ known mineral occurrences
   - SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) for transparent feature importance

4. **Prospectivity Ranking**
   - Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) to weight evidence layers
   - Grid-cell scoring across the entire AOI (cell size: 90 m × 90 m)
   - Confidence bands and uncertainty quantification

## Results

The workflow delivered high-precision prospectivity intelligence across the 500 km² area of interest with strong predictive performance (AUC 0.88–0.92).

### Quantitative Outcomes

- **Area screened**: ~500 km²
- **High-priority target zones identified**: Dozens of ranked zones across the AOI
- **Validation accuracy**: Approaching **90% hit rate** against known deposits within 1 km radius
- **Area under ROC curve (AUC)**: 0.88–0.92 (indicating strong predictive performance)
- **Top-ranked zones coverage**: ~12% of total AOI (significantly reducing exploration footprint)
- **Processing time**: 6–8 weeks from data ingestion to final deliverables
- **RMSE**: Less than 50 m for geochemical interpolation accuracy

### Deliverables Provided

1. **Fused Prospectivity Maps**: Multi-band composite highlighting alteration, structure, and geochemical anomalies
2. **Ranked Target List**: Coordinates, scores, confidence bands for priority zones
3. **Explainable AI Outputs**: SHAP feature importance showing why each target ranked high
4. **Exploration Report**: Structured document summarizing methodology, findings, limitations, and recommended next steps

### Key Insights

- Multi-sensor fusion **significantly outperformed** single-dataset approaches (e.g., optical-only or geochemistry-only)
- Structural corridors identified by radar + DEM analysis proved critical for target prioritization
- Explainable AI outputs enabled geologists to validate model reasoning against domain knowledge
- Regional-scale screening completed in **weeks**, not quarters

## Why It Matters

This type of workflow enables:

- **Rapid portfolio screening**: Compare multiple AOIs or belts before committing drill budgets (typical comparison: 3–5 regions in under 10 weeks)
- **Risk reduction**: High validation rates (88–92% AUC) translate to higher-confidence drill decisions
- **ESG alignment**: Target the best hectares first, minimizing unnecessary field disturbance (30–40% reduction in exploration footprint)
- **Cost efficiency**: Focus field programs on top-ranked zones, reducing exploration costs by 30–40% compared to broad-area surveys
