## Executive Summary

We applied our AI-MPM workflow to rapidly prioritize exploration targets. The process integrated spectral and radar indicators, yielding a ranked shortlist with field-ready candidates.

## Approach

1. Ingested and harmonized multi-sensor data
2. Applied AIG-DHA and MF/ANN fusion techniques
3. Calibrated thresholds against known occurrences
4. Produced prospectivity layers and confidence bands

## Outcome

- 4 top-ranked zones spanning ~120 km²
- Confidence bands and rationale layers for auditability
- Field planning package for rapid ground truthing
