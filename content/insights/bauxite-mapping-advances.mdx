## The Challenge in Bauxite Exploration

Traditional methods for bauxite exploration are often time-consuming and costly. They rely heavily on extensive fieldwork and sample analysis, which can be challenging in remote or inaccessible regions.

## Our AI-MPM Approach

At Scanminers, we leverage a fusion of multispectral and radar satellite imagery. Our process involves several key steps outlined in our execution plan:

1.  **Ingest & Harmonize**: We use data from Landsat, ASTER, and PALSAR.
2.  **AI & Statistical Fusion**: Techniques like AIG-DHA and MF/ANN are employed.
3.  **Rank & Validate**: We deliver prospectivity maps with clear, actionable target zones.

This method allows us to identify high-potential zones with greater accuracy and speed, significantly reducing the initial exploration footprint.
