## Integrated Data and Preprocessing

- **Multi-sensor fusion**: Combine ASTER, Sentinel-2, Landsat 8/9, and PRISMA data to capture a wide spectral range, enhancing discrimination of REE-associated minerals (e.g., carbonates, clays, Fe-oxides).
- **Spectral indices**: Derive targeted indices for carbonates, clays, and iron oxides to highlight alteration zones linked to REE mineralization.
- **Structural analysis**: Use DEM-derived lineament density maps to identify structural controls on mineralization, a critical factor in carbonatite-alkaline systems.
- **Preprocessing**: Apply atmospheric correction, Principal Component Analysis (PCA), and Minimum Noise Fraction (MNF) to reduce noise and enhance signal for machine learning input[1].

## Machine Learning Workflow

- **Algorithm selection**: Deploy Random Forest and XGBoost for their robustness with high-dimensional, geospatial data and ability to handle non-linear relationships.
- **Feature importance**: Use built-in methods to rank the contribution of spectral, structural, and geological features, informing exploration targeting.
- **Model explainability**: Integrate SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to provide transparent, interpretable outputs, increasing stakeholder confidence in model decisions[2].
- **Uncertainty quantification**: Apply bootstrap resampling and spatial simulation to quantify prediction uncertainty, critical for risk-aware decision-making[1].

## Scientific Rigor and Limitations

- **Expert review**: All workflows and outputs are subject to rigorous review by Dr. Amin Beiranvand Pour, ensuring methodological soundness and reproducibility.
- **Screening-level outputs**: Results are intended for early-stage exploration, highlighting zones warranting further ground investigation—not for resource estimation.
- **Comparison to industry standards**: Outputs do not replace NI 43-101 or JORC-compliant reports, which require detailed drilling, assaying, and economic analysis.
- **Limitations**: Reliance on remote sensing means results are sensitive to vegetation cover, cloud contamination, and sensor resolution. Ground truthing remains essential for validation.

## Key Insights

- **Advanced ML + remote sensing** can rapidly and cost-effectively screen large areas for REE potential in complex geological settings.
- **Explainability and uncertainty** metrics are now achievable, aligning with best practices in mineral exploration[1][2].
- **Integration of multi-source data** and expert oversight maximizes the scientific value of screening-level prospectivity maps, though they are not a substitute for detailed, standards-compliant resource evaluation.
